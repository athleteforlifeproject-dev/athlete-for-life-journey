Onboarding: The Comeback Test

We find out where you're at.

Before a single rep gets programmed, I need the full picture: not just what you want, but what your body's actually been through and where it stands today.

Intake: training history, injury history, goals, schedule and equipment access.

Baseline: the Comeback Test: agility, power, isometric strength, and aerobic capacity. This is the number everything else gets measured against.

App access: your account is set up. This is where every program, message, and check-in lives from here forward.

Programming Begins

Your program, not a template.

Built directly from your baseline and your goals, and built to progress with you as you improve, not a static plan you repeat for 12 weeks. Every exercise in it earns its place; nothing goes in just because it's generally "a good exercise."

ONGOING
Real-Time Coaching

Coaching, not just a program.

The plan adjusts to how you're actually responding, not a fixed script you follow blind for 12 weeks.

Biweekly calls: 20–25 minutes, every 2 weeks.

Async check-ins: ongoing messaging and form feedback in the app.

Live adjustments: movement gets corrected and re-tuned as we go.
